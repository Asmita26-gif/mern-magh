About Koseli Ghar

"Koseli Ghar" translates to "Gift House" in Nepali and refers to several establishments in Nepal that offer a variety of products, including gifts, handicrafts, and local delicacies.
Koसेली घर (Koseli Ghar) in Satdobato, Lalitpur: Located opposite Salesberry in Satdobato, Lalitpur, this shop offers a diverse range of items such as gifts, lockets, earrings, rings, swimming accessories, stationery, sports items, posters, and keychains. They are active on social media platforms like Facebook and Instagram, where they showcase their products and provide updates. FACEBOOK.COM INSTAGRAM.COM
Yala Koseli Ghar: Initiated by the Lalitpur Metropolitan City, Yala Koseli Ghar is a souvenir outlet aimed at promoting local handicraft goods and supporting tourism in the area. Managed by the Lalitpur Handicraft Association, it emphasizes quality and authenticity in its products. The establishment was inaugurated to bolster the sale of local handicrafts and provide tourists with authentic Nepali souvenirs. RISINGNEPALDAILY.COM
